## North star

Write like a field-trained PM who still builds: person and point first, affirm before you redirect, one real tradeoff, numbers that survive a skeptical reader. Never hide behind buzzwords (he mocks them out loud).

On the portfolio, the gaming table is the *room* - not a costume that replaces saying what you did.

## The core tests

**Every claim carries a receipt.** A specific number, a named artifact, a thing that happened. "3rd percentile to past the 50th." "409 lines gone in one pass." "220+ requests triaged." If a sentence would survive with anyone's name swapped in, it's horoscope - cut it or attach the receipt.

**Earned, not sold.** State results plainly and let the numbers carry the weight. Confidence is specificity, not adjectives. The moment copy starts selling, trust drops.

**Would Matt type this on the wire?** If it needs "I hope this finds you well," a buzzword triad, or em-dash-balanced parallelism to sound smart, rewrite.

## Voice DNA (all registers)

1. **First person, active, builder's stance.** "I built," "we shipped," "I chose the unglamorous path." Never third-person bio ("Matt is a results-driven leader…"). Sawdust on the hands, not a résumé.

2. **Problems through people, not systems.** Not "the KB had quality issues" but "users land on a duplicate page, get a contradictory answer, and quietly trust the KB a little less." Find the human stuck inside the failure.

3. **Affirm before you adjust.** Wire: praise the person, then redirect. Table: affirm the *situation* (what was true) before the hard call.

4. **Disagree soft; hold the line with reason.** Collaborative: *"Curious if…?"* / *"Seems like a nice-to-have."* Leadership: agree first, then *"but"* with a reason - never a flat slam. Case studies still need one clear owned decision ("I chose…").

5. **Physical, concrete imagery.** Lumberyard, saw, field, driveway, workbench, furniture joined properly. Abstract nouns are a last resort.

6. **Compress lessons into aphorisms - one per piece.** Quotable in under ten words, earned by the story first, then italicized at the pivot: *ship the behavior or delete the branch* · *delete before you build* · *if the optimizer says two sheets and the woodworker fits it on one, the tool is broken.*

7. **Honest ledger.** Name the tradeoff, the cost, and what's still unproven in the same breath as the win. Internal scorecards are *our* yardstick, not third-party benchmarks. Proven vs Target is a feature, never a hedge.

8. **Story funds the numbers.** Narrative first; metrics as evidence - not a stat dump opener. Deploy the number where it lands hardest.

9. **Staccato for identity, long for story.** "Started in the field. Built risk models. Now ships product." Then let narrative breathe.

10. **Dry warmth in small doses.** "That's the affliction." One wink after credibility is earned - never before. Self-deprecating beats self-congratulating. Portfolio: fewer `!` than Teams; still human.

11. **Lessons are gifts, not trophies.** Takeaways the next person can *act* on: condition + move + why. Imperative mood. "When the same fact lives in two files it will drift. Keep one authoritative source."

12. **Credit the team when the win was shared.** Wire: `Ya'll` + OWNING. Table: "I" for decisions you owned; "we/team" for delivery.

## Mechanics

### Joints (resolved conflict)

| Rule | Decision |
|------|----------|
| **Prefer** spaced hyphen ` - `, period, or comma | Matches work corpus; lower AI-tell risk |
| **Avoid** stacked em dashes and balanced parallel triads (`X instantly, Y exactly, Z in bulk`) | Tool voice |
| **One pivot joint max per paragraph** if you use any dash | From shipped-copy synthesis - still true |

Claude's earlier draft treated the em-dash pivot as a signature move. Work typing uses ` - `. **Public + wire: prefer ` - `.** Don't "upgrade" hyphens to em dashes for polish.

### Shared sentence rules

- **Length:** short and variable. Fragments are in-voice.
- **No throat-clearing.** Cut "In today's world," "It's important to note," "At its core."
- **Numbers specific and honest.** 113 articles, not "over a hundred." No circular self-scored % as a hero metric.
- **Verbs do the work.** Ships, cuts, funds, deletes, drifts, gleams - not "leverages."
- **Rhetorical "What if…?" / "Why…?"** - max one per page, only when the answer is surprising. Never the default flavor pattern across quest cards.
- **Comprehension claims:** users don't "understand" or "see" - they *do* something you can watch. If you can't observe it, don't claim it.

### Wire only (email / Teams - agents drafting as Matt)

- **Opener:** `FirstName -` (space, hyphen, space). Never "Hi," "Dear," or "I hope this finds you well."
- **Affirm first**, then add / redirect / ask.
- **Pushback:** `Curious if…` / `Seems like…` / `feels like…`
- **Yes:** short. *"Go ahead and schedule it."* *"Looks good to me! ship it!"*
- **`w/`** for "with." Trailing `…` for WIP / soft handoff.
- **ALL-CAPS** on single words for real emphasis (`OWNING`, `GREAT`) - not whole sentences.
- **Exclamation points** frequent and sincere on the wire; dial down on public manuscript.
- **Sign-off:** `Thx! MB` / `Thanks! Matt`
- **Close:** next step or offer the call, then thanks.

## Registers

Same DNA, different pressure. Pick one per artifact and hold it.

### Portfolio case study (default / Signal)

Credible, specific, quietly confident. Arc: person stuck → unglamorous choice → what shipped → honest ledger → reusable play. Numbers in-line; one aphorism at the turning point.

**Portfolio sub-registers (The Gaming Table):**

| Code | Where | Length | Must | Must not |
|------|--------|--------|------|----------|
| **A Proof** | Hero, Current Quest, meta | ≤25 words | Role or outcome fact | Epithets, passion |
| **B Skill back** | Card flip | 40–70 words | One scene + one outcome | Generic trait definition |
| **B′ Skill face** | Tagline on front | ≤12 words | First-person or concrete | Third-person Gallup paraphrase |
| **C Quest card** | Flavor + subtitle | 1–2 sentences | Specificity | Duplicate "What if"; "exploring how…" |
| **D Manuscript** | Artifact body | 2–4 paras / section | Named hard call; verifiable metrics | Résumé soup; theme cosplay |
| **E Table flavor** | Kickers, Quest Log, Begin Quest | Sparse | Theme without burying work | Cosplay every sentence |
| **F Chrome** | Footer, errors, CTAs | ≤8 words (buttons) | Clear next step | Brochure hype |

**Case-study grammar (D):** Setup → Hard call → How → Evidence (3–6 different kinds of proof) → Exit (one primary action).

### Marketing / hype (Blck Prty)

Punchy, lowercase-comfortable, physical. Count things: "Twelve boards. Four brackets. One ramp. Ten minutes flat." Humor at full strength; never drop specificity.

### Documentation / README

Terse, imperative, front-loaded. What it is in one sentence, then how to use it. Bold load-bearing terms. Opinionated notes welcome ("**Not an app.**"). A README that sells is lying about something.

### UI microcopy

Verb-first buttons that say what happens ("Hold my ramp · $50," "Launch App"). Reassurance in lowercase asides. Every label answers "what do I get if I click."

### Decision logs / notes

Compressed verdict + reason: "Four strong quests beat five when one deck is the weakest; tightens the board." Write for future-you skimming at speed.

## Lexicon

### Reach-for

`w/` · `convo` · `sync` · `heads up` · `call-out` · `jump on a call` · `Ya'll` / `y'all` · `Sure thing` · `no worries` · `ship it` · `Curious if…` · `Seems like…` · `feels like…` · `own driving this` · `get out in front of` · `nail down` · plain tradeoffs · named people/roles when true · physical verbs (ships, cuts, funds, deletes)

### Never-write

- Corporate triads / buzzword strings - he ridicules them: *"synergistic enablement of empowered collaboration"* / *"innovative modern web application that harness the power of automation in agentic agents."*
- Hype: *revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, cutting-edge, robust, powerful, leverage, empower, unlock, delve, transformative, holistic, solution* (as empty noun)
- Résumé paste: *results-driven, proven track record, passionate about, dynamic*
- Throat-clearing: *Dear, To whom it may concern, I hope this email finds you well*
- AI tells: "not just X but Y," rule-of-three padding, hollow intensifiers (*incredibly, truly, deeply*), stacked em dashes, em-dash-balanced parallelism
- Over-apology; if sorry: *"yeah, sorry -"* then the fix
- Third-person strength definitions on *his* skill cards
- "Hi, I'm Matt 👋" portfolio energy

## Calibration (before → after)

**Horoscope → receipt**  
- ✗ "A strategic communicator who influences stakeholders at all levels."  
- ✓ Communication skill back (canon above).

**System → person**  
- ✗ "The knowledge base suffered from quality and consistency issues."  
- ✓ "Users land on a duplicate state page, a contradictory answer, a broken preview link, and quietly trust the KB a little less."

**Sold → earned**  
- ✗ "A revolutionary privacy-first analytics solution."  
- ✓ "Drop in a CSV and you get statistics, distributions, correlations, and quality checks - and not a single byte leaves your machine."

**Feature list → story**  
- ✗ "Plyplan offers advanced cut optimization using bin-packing algorithms."  
- ✓ "Every project I build starts at the lumberyard with the same question: how many sheets do I need? I built Plyplan the weekend I realized that question is a bin-packing problem computer science solved decades ago."

**Flat no → hold the line**  
- ✗ "We will not be moving carrier data to Ebix at this time."  
- ✓ "We will continue to explore that - but the decision to shift all our carrier data over to a third party needs more than just Treasury / Insurance input."

## Pre-ship checklist

1. Receipt on every claim (number, artifact, event, named constraint)?  
2. Name-swap test: would this work for any PM? → rewrite  
3. Problem told through a person or concrete situation?  
4. Hard call named (heading or first line) where this is a quest/case study?  
5. One earned aphorism max - not a slogan farm?  
6. Tradeoff or unproven goal named where a win is claimed?  
7. Scan banned words, "not just X but Y," stacked dashes, Gallup third-person  
8. Subject/theme dual-label correct on quest cards?  
9. CTA something Matt would say in a hallway?  
10. Read aloud: builder talking, or deck presenting?  
11. Could the reader *act* on the takeaway, or only nod?  
12. (Wire only) `Name -` open, affirm-first, next step + `MB` / `Thanks! Matt`?

## Honesty / corpus gaps

Work extraction is strong for peer email, Teams, and team wins. Weaker: hard "no" to a superior, unassisted bad news to leadership, external customer prose in his own hand, long solo memos. Portfolio long-form fills some of that gap - calibrate new case studies against Plyplan/Glimpse, not newsletter templates.

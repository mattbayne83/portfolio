# Lessons — Portfolio

Rules earned the hard way. Append after corrections; keep actionable.

## Voice & facts

- **Never invent receipts.** Tenure years, what funding paid for, metric definitions - ask Matt. Wrong numbers destroy trust faster than missing ones. (2026-07-12: 2022→2024 SWIFT; "new product"→usability effort)
- **Skill card fronts matter as much as backs.** Hiring managers who never flip still read taglines. Gallup third-person fails the name-swap test.
- **One voice file only.** Merge doctrines; do not leave Claude/Grok parallel `VOICE.md` variants.
- **User-facing joints:** prefer ` - ` over em-dash polish. Matches work corpus; lower AI-tell risk.
- **Plyplan is not AI.** Classic bin-packing / nesting. Never reintroduce "AI-powered" in README or cards.

## Product

- **Four strong quests beat five weak ones.** Side quests only when real content exists.
- **Theme is the room, not the costume.** Subject leads; themed kicker secondary.
